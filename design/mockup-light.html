How itworks

How anonymity works

  • A pin is stored only as a number on a slide. No name, roll number, seat, phone or device id is saved with it.
  • Comments are stored with the slide number and the text you type. Do not write anything that could identify you.
  • When a slide has fewer than 3 pins, the lecturer sees "a few", so one student cannot be guessed.

Keyboard and screen reader

  • Press Tab to move between controls and Enter or Space to press one.
  • Pin, undo, comment and error messages are announced by a screen reader.
  • Use the text size button in the top bar to make everything larger.

کی بورڈ اور اسکرین ریڈر

  • کنٹرولز کے درمیان جانے کے لیے Tab اور دبانے کے لیے Enter یا Space استعمال کریں۔
